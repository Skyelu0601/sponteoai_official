# What is this called? — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Operator: Shanghai Zizai Era Technology Co., Ltd. (上海自在纪元科技有限公司, “SponteoAI”, “we”)

[App overview](/apps/what-is-this-called) · [Terms of Service](/terms/what-is-this-called) · [Contact us](mailto:skye@sponteoai.com)

This policy covers the What is this called? iOS app and its account, learning-sync, subscription and support services. The app is being prepared for release; this policy describes those services when available. It does not replace the policies for other SponteoAI apps. A Chinese version follows the English text.

## 1. Information we process

| Information | Why we use it |
| --- | --- |
| Account ID, sign-in provider identity, session credentials and basic profile information returned by your chosen provider | Authenticate you, secure your account and connect your devices. Google sign-in can supply your email, name and profile image. Our current native Apple sign-in does not request your name or email. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. |
| Learning language, vocabulary/review status, lesson completion, playback position, learning events and sync/device identifiers | Save progress, merge local progress after sign-in, resume lessons and synchronize your devices. |
| App user identifiers, purchased product, transaction/receipt information, subscription status, trial eligibility and expiry/renewal/refund events | Verify purchases, deliver Premium access, restore purchases and resolve billing issues through Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment-card details. |
| IP address and request metadata, app/device/OS/SDK versions, errors and service logs | Deliver downloads and online services, diagnose failures, limit abuse and protect the service. Hosting, identity and subscription providers may process these details when your device connects to them. |
| Email address and information you send when contacting support | Answer your request and resolve account or purchase problems. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers or private sign-in codes. |

Preferences, downloaded lesson media and local progress are stored on your device. Signing in enables account-based cloud synchronization. Using lessons without an account does not make every network request anonymous: downloading content and checking purchases can still involve the technical and purchase information described above, including a randomly generated subscription identifier.

The current app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. It plays supplied audio; it does not record your voice. We do not use personal learning records or Google account information to train AI models, sell personal information, or use it for targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

## 2. Sign-in and service providers

We use **Supabase** for authentication, the database and content delivery; **RevenueCat** to manage subscription access; and **Apple** for App Store purchases and optional Apple sign-in. **Google** provides optional Google sign-in. Our website is hosted by **Vercel**. Infrastructure providers process information needed to operate their services. Our API hosting provider may change; material changes to our processing will be reflected in this policy.

Google access is limited to basic sign-in identity, email and profile information. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, calendar or contacts. We use Google information only for authentication, account management, security and related support. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Service-provider policies: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). We may also disclose necessary information to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate notice and safeguards. We do not provide other users with your private learning records.

## 3. Storage, security and international processing

Our current account and learning database is located in the United States. Our company operates in China, and authorized service providers and personnel may process information in other countries. The laws in those countries may differ from your local laws. Where applicable, we use the safeguards required for international transfers and can provide further information through the contact below.

We use encrypted network connections, account access controls and restricted server credentials. Sign-in credentials are stored using the device's secure credential storage; Apple authorization tokens needed for revocation are encrypted on the server. No network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 4. Retention and deletion

We retain account and learning information while needed to provide your account and synchronize progress. Individual cloud learning-event records are scheduled for removal after 90 days; vocabulary state, completion and other cumulative progress remain available until deleted. Support, transaction and security records are kept as needed for their purpose, legal obligations and dispute resolution.

You can request account deletion in the app's account/sync screen, or contact **skye@sponteoai.com**. We may ask you to sign in again or verify ownership. Deletion removes account-linked learning records from the active learning database and queues deletion from authentication and subscription systems, including revocation of stored Apple authorization. If a provider is unavailable, the request is retried. A limited hashed deletion marker and necessary security or transaction records may remain to prevent deleted accounts from being restored and to meet legal obligations. Backup copies may remain until they expire under the applicable backup cycle and are restricted from ordinary use. Third-party providers may retain records they are independently required to keep.

Deleting the app alone does not delete your cloud account. Deleting your account does **not** cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it separately in Apple subscription settings. Local guest data and downloads can be removed by deleting the app; device or system backups are controlled through your device's backup settings.

## 5. Your choices and rights

You can use available local learning features without creating an account, sign out, manage purchases in Apple settings and disconnect Google access in your Google Account. Disconnecting a provider alone does not delete your app account. Where applicable law provides them, you may request access, a copy, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent for processing based on consent. Contact us to exercise these rights; we verify requests proportionately and respond within applicable legal time limits. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information to provide the service you request, meet legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests such as security and service reliability, or obtain consent where required. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

## 6. Young users

The service is intended for users **13 and older**, and is not directed to children under 13. Users below the age required to consent to online services in their country must have permission from a parent or guardian where required. If you believe a child under 13 has supplied personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate. An App Store content age rating is separate from these account eligibility rules.

## 7. Changes and contact

We will update the date above when this policy changes and provide additional notice for material changes when required. For privacy questions or requests, contact **skye@sponteoai.com**, Shanghai Zizai Era Technology Co., Ltd., Shanghai, China.
